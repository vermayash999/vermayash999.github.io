Our story
A warm corner
in Eberstadt
Tucked along Heidelberger Landstraße at the edge of the forest, Kaffeehaus is the kind of place you discover by chance — and return to by choice. Vintage paintings hang on every wall, artwork traces the ceiling, and the light always feels golden in the afternoon.
Whether you come for breakfast, a quiet flammkuchen, or a late evening drink, you'll find the same unhurried pace. The kind of place that doesn't rush you.
