Our Story

A culinary bridge between two great traditions

SunHee (선희) brings together the bold, communal spirit of Korean cuisine and the precise, delicate craft of Japanese cooking. Every dish is made with care, using only the freshest ingredients sourced for quality and taste.

Tucked away on Heidelberger Landstrasse in Darmstadt-Eberstadt, we offer a cozy, welcoming space for families, couples, and food lovers — whether dining in or taking away.
